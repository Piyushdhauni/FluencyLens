### Voice Conversation Performance

FluencyLens was tested across four voice conversation sessions.

| Test | Response Latency |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 11.16 s |
| 2 | 8.68 s |
| 3 | 8.78 s |
| 4 | 8.33 s |

**Average response latency: 9.24 seconds**

The latency was measured manually from pressing the Stop button to hearing the beginning of the AI-generated voice response.

*Note: These are preliminary measurements from four manual tests, not a formal performance benchmark.*
